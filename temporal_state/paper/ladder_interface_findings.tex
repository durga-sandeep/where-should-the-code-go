Table~\ref{tab:interaction_canonical} crosses requested effort with the tool-calling interface. For DeepSeek V4 Flash on its provider the forced call effectively disabled reasoning: forced canonical calls averaged 32 reasoning tokens against 1695 under automatic choice, and accuracy is 2.1 against 99.0\%; effort changes nothing under either interface. For GPT-5.4 mini and for DeepSeek V4 Pro on its provider the forced call interacts with effort: mini's high-minus-low effect on canonical input is -41.1 [-51.0, -31.2] under the forced call and +6.8 [+0.5, +13.5] under automatic choice, and DeepSeek V4 Pro falls from 98.4 to 82.3\% under the forced call at high effort (32 of 192 calls with no answer) while staying at 97.4\% under automatic choice. GPT-5.4 shows a small interaction of the same sign (+5.2 [+1.0, +9.4]). For gpt-oss-20b, gpt-oss-120b, Qwen3.8-27B and GLM-5.3 Flash the interaction interval covers zero: effort acts the same way under both interfaces. A third failure of \emph{reason harder} is budget exhaustion: at high effort gpt-oss-120b spent the whole 16,384-token output limit on reasoning in 65 of 192 forced canonical calls and gpt-oss-20b in 97 of 192 automatic raw calls, each a missing answer. gpt-oss-20b also refused the fictional task (88 of 192 low-effort raw calls had no answer, mostly refusals), so its cells measure willingness as much as ability.
