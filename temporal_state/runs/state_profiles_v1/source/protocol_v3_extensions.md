# Extension studies: reasoning effort and prospective state-only reconstruction

Frozen locally on 7 September 2026 (Pacific time), before any call for these studies. The original four final cohorts (4,224 requests), their frozen source snapshots, analyses, audits and the post-hoc analyses are unchanged. These two studies were motivated by skeptical review of the completed manuscript, so they are extension studies designed after seeing the original outcomes; they are prospective relative to this document and confirmatory relative to the two hypotheses stated below. They reuse the original heldout histories, so they do not add independent histories.

## Reviewer objections addressed

1. **Compute-budget objection.** The largest failures belong to GPT-5.4 mini at low reasoning effort, whose median response used about 570 output tokens. A reviewer may attribute the raw-history failure and the residual rollout error to a small reasoning budget rather than to the task structure. Study E1 raises the model's own reasoning effort while holding representation fixed, so that "more model computation" can be compared directly with "code before the model" (canonical, snapshot) as remedies.
2. **Post-hoc pipeline objection.** The executable-continuation result was computed offline from five-field answers. The original prompt also demanded a future rollout, which could have consumed capacity needed for reconstruction, or conversely could have helped the model organize its replay. Study E2 prompts the model for the two historical fields only and executes the supplied future schedule in code. This is the actual hybrid pipeline the continuation analysis approximated.

## Study E1: reasoning-effort sweep

- Configurations: `mini_medium` and `mini_high`, identical to `mini_low` (`gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17`) except for OpenAI `reasoning.effort` set to `medium` or `high`. Same instructions, strict five-integer schema, forced `submit_answer`, 16,384 total output tokens, 420-second timeout, no retry or repair.
- Arms: raw, canonical, snapshot, exactly as in the original study.
- Tasks: the 96 heldout synthetic histories from `runs/heldout_v2/manifest.json` and the 48 heldout retail-profile windows from `runs/profiles_v2/manifest.json`, read through `--source-run`, so every task is byte-identical to its original. Two repetitions each.
- Requests: 2 × 3 × 96 × 2 = 1,152 synthetic; 2 × 3 × 48 × 2 = 576 profile; 1,728 total.
- Primary endpoints: future-joint exact accuracy and starting-stock exact accuracy per configuration/arm. Secondary: all-five accuracy, historical joint, binary stockout false alarms and misses, invalid and truncated counts, output tokens and cost per call.
- Prespecified comparisons: (a) within each arm, medium minus low and high minus low, paired by base with repetitions averaged within base, 10,000 base-bootstrap percentile intervals; (b) the raw-arm gain from raising effort low→high versus the low-effort gain from raw→canonical and raw→snapshot, presented side by side with observed token cost per call. Intervals are descriptive and unadjusted.
- Hypothesis H1 (stated in advance): higher effort improves mini's raw and canonical accuracy, but at fixed effort the snapshot arm remains at or above the raw arm at every effort level, and residual rollout error (future-joint below 100% in the snapshot arm) persists at medium effort. We do not predict whether high effort eliminates residual rollout error; either result is reported. Configurations remain not compute-matched; effort is a requested setting, not an observed token ceiling.

## Study E2: prospective state-only reconstruction with executable continuation

- Arms: `state_raw` and `state_canonical`. Inputs are identical to raw and canonical except that `future_schedule` and `horizon_time` are omitted. The model returns exactly two nonnegative integers, `stock_at_target` and `historical_lost`, through a strict two-field `submit_answer` schema. Instructions are the original instructions with the future-scenario paragraph replaced by an explicit statement that no future scenario is to be simulated (`STATE_INSTRUCTIONS` in `run.py`).
- Executable continuation: code applies the original supplied future schedule to the emitted stock with the reference simulator, producing a five-field hybrid answer. The stored `score` is the ordinary five-field score of that hybrid answer; a hybrid answer is valid only if the two-field answer is valid. Both the two-field answer and the hybrid answer are stored in every episode record.
- Configurations: `mini_low`, `gpt54_low`, `sonnet46`, `sonnet46_thinking` (the original four), plus `mini_high` so that representation, effort and rollout placement can be read jointly for the weakest configuration. Snapshot has no state-only counterpart because its state is already supplied.
- Tasks: the same 96 synthetic and 48 profile heldout tasks via `--source-run`; two repetitions.
- Requests: 5 × 2 × 96 × 2 = 1,920 synthetic; 5 × 2 × 48 × 2 = 960 profile; 2,880 total.
- Primary endpoints: hybrid future-joint accuracy (equal to state-only stock accuracy by conservation, which is verified for every episode) and hybrid all-five accuracy, compared with the original five-field model rollout in the corresponding arm and with the post-hoc continuation estimate.
- Prespecified comparisons, all paired by base with repetitions matched by index: (a) state-only stock accuracy minus original five-field stock accuracy in the same arm and configuration; (b) hybrid future-joint accuracy minus original model future-joint accuracy; (c) hybrid future-joint accuracy minus post-hoc continuation accuracy (which is the original five-field stock accuracy). Comparison (c) tests whether the post-hoc estimate was faithful.
- Hypothesis H2 (stated in advance): state-only stock accuracy is within a few points of the original five-field stock accuracy for every configuration and arm (the post-hoc estimate is faithful), and the prospective hybrid therefore matches or exceeds original model rollout wherever continuation helped post hoc. A large positive difference in (a) would instead show that the five-field burden depressed reconstruction; a large negative difference would show that requesting the rollout helped reconstruction. All three outcomes are reported.

## Shared rules

- Frozen before dispatch: this document, `run.py`, `benchmark.py`, `external_benchmark.py`, and the transport module, all snapshotted with SHA-256 hashes in each run manifest.
- Dispatch: one runner at a time under the shared lock, 48 worker threads, at most 24 in-flight requests per provider. Jobs are shuffled by seed. A transport or provider error interrupts the run and preserves every completed trace; interrupted runs are reported as such and not silently replaced.
- Budget: the shared conservative ledger with the existing $3,000 cumulative ceiling; prior recorded and reserved cost is about $210. Worst-case reservations are computed per call; no automatic escalation.
- Smoke check before the studies: a development-split check of at most 12 calls (two development histories, `mini_medium`, `mini_high` and `sonnet46`, arms raw and `state_raw`, one repetition) verifies that the provider accepts the effort settings and the two-field schema. It is excluded from all estimates.
- Analysis: `extension_analysis.py` reparses provider responses independently of stored scores, verifies the full planned grid, recomputes truth with the independent auditor's replay, and checks the conservation identity on every hybrid answer before producing tables. Original heldout cells are read from the archived runs. All configurations, arms, invalid answers and regressions are reported. There is no expansion gate: both studies are reported whatever they show.
- Not claimed: compute matching across providers or effort levels; that requested effort equals used reasoning tokens; that a two-field prompt is a deployed system; independence of repeated requests on the same history; realism of synthetic reporting delays.

## Amendment 1: contemporaneous low-effort replication (written before its calls)

Added after E1's synthetic cohort completed and before any further analysis of it. E1 compares new medium/high-effort calls with low-effort calls collected several days earlier. Although the model identifier is dated and unchanged, a provider-side serving change could confound the comparison. A fifth run, `replication_mini_low_v1`, therefore repeats `mini_low` on the 96 synthetic heldout histories, all three arms, two repetitions (576 calls), with the identical frozen prompts and tasks, dispatched after the four planned runs. Its purpose is a same-period baseline: if it reproduces the original low-effort cells within ordinary repeat variation, the effort comparison stands; if it does not, the effort comparison is reported against the contemporaneous baseline and the drift is disclosed. Both original and replication cells are reported either way. This run is not pooled into the original heldout estimates.
